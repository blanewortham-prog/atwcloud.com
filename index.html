Sound Familiar?

Your pricing strategy is only as good as the spreadsheet it lives in.

Most distributors didn't choose Excel as their pricing system—it just became one. Price changes get modeled in offline grids, emailed for approval, then keyed line by line into the ERP. It works, until the business outgrows it.

⌨️

Manual keying into the ERP

Analysts re-type approved prices into condition records by hand. Every keystroke is a chance for a margin-killing error.

🗂️

Version chaos

"FINAL_v7_updated.xlsx" lives on someone's desktop. Nobody is certain which grid is the source of truth.

✉️

Approvals by email

Price decisions are approved in inbox threads with no audit trail, no guardrails, and no visibility into who changed what.

⏳

Slow speed to market

A price move that should take a day takes weeks to model, approve, load, and verify—while competitors and suppliers keep moving.

🕳️

Invisible margin leakage

Keying errors, expired deals, and missed chargebacks quietly bleed margin, and nobody finds out until month-end—if ever.

🧮

Analysts doing data entry

Your most commercially sharp people spend their week maintaining grids instead of analyzing price, mix, and competitive position.

The fix isn't a better spreadsheet. It's a pricing system that models scenarios, routes approvals, and posts directly to your ERP—selected, built, tested, and launched by people who have run pricing inside a large distributor.

FAQ

Common questions.

Our Excel pricing models work. Why change?

The models usually aren't the problem—the handoffs are. Every time a price moves from a spreadsheet into the ERP by hand, you add keying errors, lag, and lost audit trail. We keep the logic your team trusts and remove the manual re-entry.

Should we buy a pricing platform or build something custom?

It depends on your pricing complexity, ERP, budget, and internal IT capacity. We run a structured build-vs-buy evaluation across enterprise pricing platforms, ERP-native pricing, and custom builds, and recommend the option that fits—we don't resell software.

Which ERPs do you work with?

Our deepest experience is SAP, including S/4HANA pricing condition records and Settlement Management. For other ERPs we design the integration through standard interfaces and work alongside your IT team or systems integrator.

Can you get our pricing into e-commerce, EDI, and reporting systems too?

Yes—that's a dedicated phase of our roadmap. We design how current, future-dated, and historical pricing flows from your pricing system and ERP to e-commerce and B2B portals, customer EDI price catalogs, sales apps, BI tools, and data repositories used for analytics and AI, with clear ownership and reconciliation.

How long does a pricing technology transformation take?

A diagnostic takes a few weeks. Vendor selection commonly runs two to three months. Full implementation typically runs several months to a year depending on scope, number of pricing programs, and integration complexity.

Can you step into a project that's already underway?

Yes. We frequently join mid-stream to rescue stalled UAT, untangle integration issues, or give the business side an independent voice alongside the implementation partner.
